Privacy notice

AccessLens is a public StudioNet demonstration. Do not submit private URLs, passwords, personal information, or confidential text in the optional focus field.

What becomes public

When an audit finalizes, the target URL, optional focus, wallet address, scores, finding excerpts, page and full-report digests, capture coverage, and any caller-selected comparison link are stored on GenLayer StudioNet. These records are publicly readable and cannot be removed by the AccessLens website. Validators also fetch and process the submitted public page to produce the report. The full rendered HTML is not archived by the contract, but the submitted URL and excerpts may themselves contain personal information.

Temporary wallet

If you choose the instant Studio wallet, its signing key is generated in your browser and kept in that tab's session storage. It is not sent to the AccessLens website operator. Closing the tab can end access to that temporary key; public audit records remain readable.

Website operation

The app does not require an account, request personal details, or run its own report database. Its hosting provider and the GenLayer network may process ordinary technical request metadata to deliver the site and transactions. The app does not include a separate analytics tracker.

Contact

AccessLens is built and operated by Leokings. For questions or privacy concerns, open a GitHub issue without including sensitive information.
