Use terms

AccessLens provides evidence-backed interface feedback, not a legal accessibility certification or a guarantee that a website is safe or compliant.

Appropriate use

Submit only public HTTPS pages that you are permitted to review. Do not submit private systems, sensitive information, or content you do not have a right to share. The URL and optional focus text become public when an audit finalizes.

How reports work

GenLayer validators independently render the target page and make contextual judgments. Public pages may change, block automation, or render differently between validators. An audit can fail without a stored report, and two audits of the same page may differ. Treat findings as suggestions to verify, not as definitive facts about every visitor's experience.

Availability and cost

The current StudioNet flow is gasless and requires no payment to AccessLens. The demonstration and network may be unavailable or change. A temporary Studio wallet is not intended for holding funds.
